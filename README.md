# Backend Engineering Fundamentals

*The Building Blocks of Every Backend, from the Request to Production*

A 299-page book of study notes on a backend engineering video course. It covers videos 5–30 of the playlist.

**[Read or download the PDF](Backend-Engineering-Fundamentals.pdf)**

## What each chapter contains

- A one-page overview of the lecture.
- A two-column paper that covers the lecture, with definitions, notes, warnings, figures and tables. Where the lecture needs a correction, a box titled "A subtle point" gives it.
- **Appendix A:** eight high-level review questions, without answers.
- **Appendix B:** a glossary of the chapter's terms.
- **Appendix C:** further topics that the lecture did not explain.

The references for all chapters are collected at the end of the book.
